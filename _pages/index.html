About

I am Henry Moss, an Associate Professor (UK Reader) in Mathematics and AI in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Lancaster University, where I hold a UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship and lead a team working on Mathematical AI for Decision and Discovery. I am part of MARS, Lancaster's centre for the mathematics of AI in real-world systems.

AI has dramatically changed how scientists approach experimentation, but there remains a need for smarter models to accelerate the pace of innovation — particularly where experiments are technically and financially costly. We have reached a point where the classical mathematical principles of experimental design can no longer keep pace with modern science.

The aim of the Mathematical AI for Decision and Discovery (MADD) Group is to work closely with industry partners to remove innovation bottlenecks in areas such as drug discovery and sustainable engineering. We are experts in probabilistic machine learning, generative modelling, Gaussian processes, Bayesian optimisation, active learning and experimental design.

Our work has been recognised at the main machine learning venues: a Best Paper Award at AISTATS 2026, together with spotlight at at ICML 2025, an oral at AISTATS 2023 and a spotlight at NeurIPS 2020

Before Lancaster I was an Assistant Research Professor in DAMTP at Cambridge, a research scientist at Secondmind, and a machine learning consultant to AstraZeneca. In 2021, I graduated from Lancaster's STOR-i with a PhD in statistical machine learning, after reading mathematics at Emmanuel College, Cambridge.
